Leadership • Mbale Media Daily

Musimbi Gerald

Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

Musimbi Gerald is the Chief Executive Officer of Mbale Media Daily, a digital news platform focused on bringing timely, relevant and trusted stories from Mbale, Eastern Uganda, Uganda and the wider world.

Mbale Media Daily was founded on 19 March 2025 with a mission to inform communities, amplify local voices and make reliable news accessible to people in Mbale, Eastern Uganda and beyond.

His multidisciplinary academic background spans Journalism, Information and Communication Technology (ICT), Coffee Agronomy and Theology.

His leadership is centered on responsible storytelling, digital media growth and giving communities a platform to be heard.

Our Mission

Mbale Media Daily exists to inform, connect and empower communities through accurate, timely and responsible journalism. We are committed to amplifying local voices, covering the issues that matter to people in Mbale and Eastern Uganda, and bringing our audience trusted national, regional and global news.

Our mission is rooted in responsible storytelling, community service, transparency and the belief that Every Story Matters.
